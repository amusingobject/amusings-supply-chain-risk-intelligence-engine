自由港區業務

自由貿易港區事業「籌設」許可審查作業流程 字級： 小 中 大

發布日期： 2018-11-09 |　觀看數： 2653 |　維護單位： 港務組 |　最後檢核時間： 2026-01-13 |　最後修改時間： 2026-01-13
分享至臉書 分享至推特 分享至噗浪


本作業係依據自由貿易港區事業營運管理辦法第3、4及5條，得申請經營港區事業業務（含港區貨棧），從事貿易、倉儲、物流、貨櫃（物）集散、轉口、轉運、承攬運送、報關服務、組裝、重整、包裝、修理、裝配、加工、製造、檢驗、測試、展覽或技術服務等事業。

 一、 申請文件

申請經營港區事業，應檢具下列文件，向管理機關提出籌設許可申請：

    (一)  申請書。

    (二)  營運計畫書。

    (三)  貨物控管、貨物電腦連線通關及帳務處理作業說明書。

    (四)  設施或土地租賃契約書或相關證明文件。

    (五)  申請人證明文件：

        1.公司登記證明文件。

        2.新投資創設者，其公司名稱、所營事業登記預查核准文件及下列代表人證件：

            (1)本國人：國民身分證影本。

            (2)僑民：僑民身分證明文件。

            (3)外國人：國籍證明書或當地國所核發之護照影本。

    (六)  經營業務須經特許者應檢附相關證明文件。

    (七)  授權代理人辦理者，應檢附代理人授權書及代理人身分證影本或居留證影本。

二、補正程序

申請籌設應具備文件不全或其記載內容不完備者，管理機關將通知申請人於30日內補正；屆期未補正或補正而仍不完備者，駁回其申請。


This procedure is based on Articles 3, 4, and 5 of the "Regulations Governing the Operation and Management of Free Trade Zones Enterprises", an enterprise that applies to operate a business within an FTZ to engage in the business activities related to trading, warehousing, logistics, collecting and distributing (cargo of) containers, transiting, transshipment, forwarding, customs clearance, assembling, sorting, packaging, repairing, configuring, processing, manufacturing, examining, testing, displaying or technological service.

  • 1. Application Documents

    When applying for operating within an FTZ, an enterprise shall submit the documents below to apply with the Management Authority for an establishment permit:

    • (1) An application
    • (2) A business plan
    • (3) The operating procedure description for goods control, the on-line operations of goods customs clearance and accounting operations
    • (4) Facility or land lease contract or relevant evidentiary documents
    • (5) Identification documents of the applicant：
      • A. Registration document of the Company;
      • B. For a newly established enterprise, the name of the company, pre-approved document of business registration, and identification documents of representatives below are required:
        • (A) A citizen of the ROC: A photocopy of the National Identification Card
        • (B) Overseas compatriots: Identification document of overseas compatriots
        • (C) Foreign national: Nationality document or a photocopy of passport issued by investor’s home country
    • (6) Business in operation permitted shall be attached with the relevant documents.
    • (7) If the said application is authorized to be filed by an authorized attorney, the power of attorney issued to such attorney together with a photocopy of the attorney’s identification card or Certificate of Residency shall be submitted.
    • 2. Correction Procedure

    If the supporting documents submitted along with the application are found to be incomplete or contain incomplete information, the Management Authority shall require the applicant through a notice in writing to rectify within thirty (30) days. If within the prescribed period the applicant fails to rectify or make complete rectification, the application will be rejected.
